# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not.

## Scope

This applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces moderated by the project maintainers.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Security vulnerabilities belong under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in conduct reports.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to keep project spaces safe and useful.
